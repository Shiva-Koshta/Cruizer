Cruizer drives the Royal Enfield Tripper Pod on your motorcycle's dial with
turn-by-turn directions — without Royal Enfield's own app, an account, or an
internet connection. Your navigation app already knows where to turn; Cruizer
relays those directions to the Pod's screen.

<b>What you get</b>

• Turn arrows on the dial: left/right, slight, sharp, U-turn, roundabouts with the exit number
• Distance to the turn counting down; the bottom line can show remaining route distance, arrival time, or alternate between them
• Live ride stats in the app: time, distance, speeds, an estimated fuel figure (GPS optional — the clock works without location permission)
• Multiple saved bikes (engine size, economy) for the fuel estimate
• Day/night mode for the Pod that follows the sun's position, or manual

<b>⚠️ Status: early, expect bugs</b>

Cruizer works, but it is <b>not stable software yet</b>. It speaks a
reverse-engineered protocol and reads other apps' notifications — corner
cases will misbehave, and significant bugs are likely. Verify it while
parked and be ready to ignore the Pod if the screen goes wrong mid-ride.

<b>How it works</b>

1. Turn the ignition on. The Pod advertises over Bluetooth for about 30 seconds.
2. Press Connect in Cruizer and type the 6-digit PIN the Pod shows — the phone is remembered for later rides.
3. Start navigation in your app (CoMaps, OsmAnd, Organic Maps, Google Maps, Waze). Cruizer reads its turn instructions — structured data where available, notifications otherwise (Google Maps only updates its notification about once a minute) — and sends them to the Pod about once per second.

Nothing leaves your phone: no cloud, no account, no tracking.

<b>Requirements</b>

• An Android 12+ phone with Bluetooth
• A Royal Enfield Tripper Pod (Meteor 350 etc.). The newer Tripper Dash cluster (Hunter 350 etc.) is a different device and is not supported yet.
• Optional: location permission for the ride distance/speed and the day/night mode
